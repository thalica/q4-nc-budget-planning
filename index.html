01 · Strategic diagnosisWHERE WE ARE

H1 already told us where the money works. Q4 executes it.

01

What worked

  • Content co-created with resellers and etailers produced 1M+ views in Colombia, with 85–97% of reach coming from non-followers.
  • The Mercado Libre visibility pilot moved 15K sales.
  • PC Factory's digital catalog returned 315K views a month.
02

What did not

  • Open events without segmentation drew prize-hunters, not buyers.
  • Mass floordays with large sales teams diluted the result and never showed up in billing.
  • Leads were never captured anywhere along the chain.
03

Opportunity

  • Laptops and OLED monitors carried the traction of the half; VGA left the focus on a projected stock shortage.
  • In Peru the physical point of sale still closes the purchase, and that is exactly where the brand is barely visible.
  • Resellers already produce their own content; what is missing is media behind it.
04

Q4 implication

  • 48% of the budget goes to the shelf where the laptop is bought.
  • 19% goes behind content the resellers already make.
  • No event is approved without a defined audience: tournaments qualify on sales targets.
  • MB and VGA receive no budget and no MDF claim this quarter.

Pixie Tec — new GIGABYTE tech reviewer

Build authentic product credibility among Peru's DIY PC-building and student tech audience.

$1,297NovemberViews 100K
How

The Gaming A16 goes inside her "how to build a PC" format, turning the placement into product education.

It is a series her audience already trusts for unbiased hardware guidance, so GIGABYTE's credibility moves from spec sheets to real product experience. Three videos, one per month.

Why

Real trust inside the DIY PC-building niche, at a fraction of a macro-influencer's cost.

That trust is rare for a mid-size creator. Her tutorial format works as organic product education for viewers who research before they buy. Product focus: GIGABYTE GAMING A16 & AERO X16.

THE THREE CONTENTS
  • Gaming A16 featured within her existing "how to build a PC" series
  • "AERO X16 vs. desktop" — she builds a desktop PC for gaming and study while testing the A16 side by side, showing it holds up without the setup
  • Day-in-the-life integration — the GAMING A16 shown as her go-to device for editing, studying and gaming in her everyday content, not just a dedicated review
